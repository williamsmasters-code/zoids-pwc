Zaber Fang and Gojulas use the built-in procedural mocks. Death Stinger and
Death Saurer have procedural fallbacks for ?mock=1.

Gun Sniper, Elephander, Berserk Fuhrer, Molga, and Pteras are original
procedural models built for this project (js/models.js). No third-party
assets were used for them. No free CC-BY or CC0 model of these Zoids was
found on Sketchfab, Poly Haven or OpenGameArt (searched 21 Oct 2026).
Open the game with ?mock=1 to force the mocks for every Zoid.
