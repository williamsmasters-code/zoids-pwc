1. ROOT CAUSES (WITH EVIDENCE)

Measured from the real GLB part meshes in the mission (not the catalog radius):

  Gun Sniper   0.78 m wide head-on overall, core only 0.40 m wide, 2.49 m tall
  Command Wolf 1.34 m wide     Iron Kong 4.38 m wide

a) The lock point sat in empty air. The old lock / lead point was 1.64 m above
   the root of a Gun Sniper, above its thin body. A camera ray through that
   point missed the model and hit the ground far behind it: target at 25 m ->
   ray hit at 53 m, 50 m -> 146 m, 100 m -> 253 m, 150 m -> 497 m. A level ray
   at that height hit nothing. Other Zoids are wide enough that the same ray
   still struck them.
b) Convergence on the far ground. Every mount aimed at the world point under
   the crosshair. With the ray passing the Gun Sniper, that point was 50-350 m
   behind it, so off-centre mounts (shoulder beam cannons, side guns)
   converged behind the target and their shots passed either side of a 0.4 m
   core (parallax). Shield Liger beam cannon: 0% at every range; at 120 m all
   90 ticks "sailed past".
c) Hits used the render meshes. Thin barrels and sticks were paper, and the
   core box was the narrow real core.
d) The AI and missiles used other heights. AI aim: root + radius x 0.45 =
   1.03 m. Missile seeker: radius x 0.4 = 0.92 m. Neither matched the lock box.
e) Missiles at close range climbed over a short target and dived into the
   ground: Shield Liger missiles 0/8 at 25 m and 0/8 at 50 m, all "ground".
   Against Iron Kong they also missed at 120-160 m (overshoot then dive).
f) Swept-segment order. A projectile step that ended below the terrain was
   counted as a ground hit before the step was tested against units. A tall
   shooter (Gojulas back gun 9 m up) firing down at a short Gun Sniper passed
   through it and into the ground in the same 0.05 s step. Gojulas sniper
   rifle: 0/4 at 50 m, all "ground"; it went to 4/4 once the unit test runs
   first. (Found during the after-measurement.)
g) Gatling spread. Its designed cone (0.03 rad + up to 0.045 bloom, wider past
   its 35 m ideal) is wider than a Gun Sniper past about 25 m. That is a design
   property, not a bug; see judgment calls.


2. HIT VOLUMES AND AIM POINT (js/targeting.js)

  Each Zoid is hit-tested against compound boxes in its own root frame, built
  once at spawn from its real part meshes: one box per mesh, padded 0.06 m a
  side, at least 0.22 m thick on every axis. The largest core box is widened
  to at least 0.5 x radius wide and deep and 0.42 x radius tall, and lifted so
  it never sinks below the model's lowest point. Big Zoids already exceed that
  floor, so they keep their own volume (Iron Kong is only padded). Gun Sniper
  core box: 0.40 m -> 1.15 m wide.
  Centre of mass: volume-weighted centre of the body boxes (core and head 1,
  legs 0.35, weapons and tail 0), moved inside a core/head box if it falls in
  a gap. Gun Sniper aim point: 1.02 m above the root (was 1.64 m).
  Shots: bounding-sphere reject, then the boxes. The three.js raycaster on
  render meshes is no longer used for unit hits.


3. LOCK ASSIST, LEAD, ZEROING

  Lock range 190 m, the 12 degree R box, R lock / Tab cycle, falloff numbers:
  unchanged.
  Assist (modest, only on the locked target): if the crosshair ray passes
  within snap = 0.3 + 0.22 x radius m of the centre of mass (Gun Sniper
  0.81 m), the aim point is the centre of mass. Within the lock box
  (0.6 + 0.75 x radius m) the player's own line is kept but the convergence
  point is set at the target's range, so mounts converge on the target and not
  on the ground behind it. Outside that, the crosshair rules as before.
  The lead box, the IMPACT diamond and ballistic zeroing are solved from the
  selected weapon's own muzzle to the centre of mass (height included).


4. MISSILES

  Pop-up time = clamp(0.1 + d / 220, 0.16 s, stock pop) and dive bias =
  clamp(0.2 x d, 4, 22) m, from the launch range d. Terminal homing gets
  1 + 2 x (1 - distance / 35) inside 35 m. Proximity fuse after the 25 m
  arming range: 0.6 + 0.12 x radius m from the hit volume. Missiles home on
  the centre of mass. The 2.2 s lock, the cloud rules and the 25 m arming
  rule are unchanged.


5. PER-HARDPOINT AIM AND WEAPON SELECTION

  Every weapon fires from its own muzzle. Aim, lead, zero, convergence and
  the line-of-sight check (AIM / BLOCKED mark) are solved per muzzle.
  The trigger fires only the selected weapon.
    Mouse wheel  down = next weapon, up = previous, wraps around (primary)
    1-9          select that slot directly (secondary)
    Left mouse   fire the selected weapon
    Right mouse  sniper weapon selected: scope zoom (see 7); any other weapon:
                 a second trigger for the selected weapon
  Slot order: head, left, right, back, then the standard melee last.
  Switching takes 0.18 s (the dock row reads SWITCHING). Unselected weapons
  keep cooling and reloading, so switching between a missile salvo and a
  beam during its cooldown pays off.
  The reticle shows the selected weapon's spread circle, rocket cone, impact,
  drop, lead, range band and melee reach. Pips around the reticle, one per
  slot, mark the selected slot. The weapon dock has one row per slot (heat,
  cooldown, state, max range) with the selected row highlighted.
  The wheel did not zoom in battle before, so nothing had to move. The hangar
  viewer wheel zoom is unchanged.
  Turrets keep their own traverse and aim from their own barrel to the
  centre of mass, but now fire only when selected with the trigger held
  (they no longer fire on their own).
  The fire modes (gatling spin-up, pulse bursts, sustained beam, salvo, charge)
  are unchanged.
  Legacy weapon groups still exist for scripted input and tests only.
  No gamepad or touch input exists in this game, so there is no left-trigger
  scope binding.


6. STANDARD MELEE

  Every combat Zoid has a free, always-fitted melee weapon, last in its slot
  list. No ammo. Heat 4 per swing, cooling 18/s. 0.8 swings per second.
  Reach = 4 + 2.4 x radius m, measured from the Zoid's contact point (the front
  of its hit volume at centre-of-mass height) to the target's hit volume.
  It must be inside the style's arc and in line of sight (cover blocks a
  swing). Damage = 10 + 0.12 x base weight (Blade Liger x1.15, energy).
  A swing lunges forward (lunge x 4.5 m/s for 0.22 s). A hit knocks the
  target back (5 m/s x weight ratio, clamped 0.35-2.2) and staggers it
  0.3-0.5 s, with an impact flash and a hit sound. A swing that is out of
  reach still swings and lunges, but does no damage.
  The reticle shows IN REACH / OUT OF REACH and the distance.
  If the striking part is destroyed, the melee is lost.

  Balance: melee DPS sits at about half of the best single ranged weapon
  (20-35 DPS) in exchange for no heat trouble, no ammo and the knockback /
  stagger. The bought melee weapons (Strike Laser Claw, Laser Blade) are
  unchanged and stronger.

  Animations
    Shield Liger: a real skinned clip, "Melee" (8 bones, 16 keys at 24 fps,
      0.62 s), baked into assets/models/shield-liger-anim.glb by
      tools/add_melee_clip.mjs with gltf-transform. Meshopt compression and
      the existing clips and credits are kept. It plays additively over the
      walk / idle like the Fire clip. Verified in the browser: the right
      front paw goes back and up in the wind-up (root-local z -1.43 ->
      -0.77 m), then forward and up in the strike (z -3.44 m, y 1.29 m).
    Every other Zoid: procedural motion. A whole-body rig (lunge, pitch,
      roll, yaw) plus part swings about pivots on the real part meshes:
      claw = front leg / arm raise and slash; bite = head rear-up then snap;
      blade = blades sweep in, head dips (Blade Liger); fist = arm raised
      overhead and slammed down (Iron Kong); tail = tail and body whip
      sideways (Gojulas); kick = leg cocked back then kicked forward (Gun
      Sniper); ram = head-down charge (Elephander, Molga); sting = tail over
      the back (Death Stinger).
    Why not more skinned clips: only the Shield Liger GLB is rigged. The
    others are static part meshes, so a skinned clip would need a new rig per
    model. Procedural part motion was used for those instead.


7. SNIPER SCOPE (RIGHT MOUSE)

  With a sniper-class weapon selected (the Sniper Rifle, or any weapon marked
  scope), holding RMB zooms smoothly (0.18 s) to 4x (FOV 60 -> 15). A second
  right click while held steps to 8x (FOV 7.5). The view moves to the cockpit
  eye while zoomed so the player's own hull never fills a third-person scope.
  A scope ring overlay shows the zoom, the range and DROP / ZERO; the reticle,
  lead box and impact mark stay. Look sensitivity is divided by zoom^0.8
  (4x = 0.33 of normal). Releasing RMB, or switching weapon with the wheel
  or a number key, leaves the scope. RMB held through a switch is latched: it
  neither re-zooms nor fires the new weapon until released. The browser
  context menu stays suppressed. RMB on a non-sniper weapon fires it.


8. AI PARITY

  The AI aims at the same centre of mass, from each weapon's own muzzle, with
  the same spread, drop, falloff and lock rules. It fires one weapon at a
  time: every 0.5 s it picks the slot with the best
  DPS x range-damage x heat factor x hit chance (the spread cone at that
  range against the target's size), charge weapons x0.2 while moving. The
  standard melee wins outright when it is in reach. Example: a Molga shooting
  a Pteras 18 m up and 45 m out now uses its pulse laser instead of a gatling
  that cannot hit it. AI turrets fire only when the AI has selected them.

10. TIME TO KILL AND BALANCE

  tools/ttk_probe.mjs: stock loadout, a stationary dummy, 12 s of fire,
  damage per second on target (target armour classes applied).
  Before: "LMB" = the old group 1 trigger, "all" = all four groups held.
  After: the best single slot (switching not used).

  shooter -> target, range    before LMB / all     after best slot
  Shield Liger -> Gun Sniper 25   13.9 / 17.6      25.5 (beam cannon)
  Shield Liger -> Gun Sniper 60   16.2 / 16.1      25.5
  Shield Liger -> Iron Kong 25    19.5 / 45.0      25.5
  Shield Liger -> Iron Kong 60    18.8 / 61.0      25.5
  Gun Sniper -> Gun Sniper 25      0 / 7.9         21.2 (sniper rifle)
  Gun Sniper -> Iron Kong 60      33.3 / 61.4      23.3
  Iron Kong -> Gun Sniper 25      19.2 / 17.6      26.8 (shock cannon)
  Iron Kong -> Iron Kong 60       46.9 / 59.6      25.3
  Gojulas -> Gun Sniper 25        32.8 / 35.2      34.7 (gatling)
  Gojulas -> Iron Kong 25         93.2 / 93.2      34.7
  Command Wolf -> Gun Sniper 60   17.3 / 16.8      28.5 (double beam)
  Command Wolf -> Iron Kong 25    76.2 / 78.0      34.7

  Result: against the Gun Sniper, time to kill falls by 1.0-3x for most
  shooters (it could barely be hit before). Against big Zoids, a pilot who
  fired everything at once deals 1.8-2.7x less damage per second with one
  weapon, so those fights take about twice as long. The AI fires one weapon
  at a time as well, so the change is symmetric and the player-versus-AI
  balance holds.
  Decision: per-weapon damage, cooldowns and heat are unchanged (the weapons
  overhaul rules asked to keep DPS). The switch is fast (0.18 s) and
  unselected weapons keep reloading, so a pilot who weaves weapons gets
  part of the old total back. If fights feel slow in play, the one-line
  option is a global x1.3 damage multiplier on ranged weapons for player and
  AI alike. It is proposed, not applied.

12. JUDGMENT CALLS AND KNOWN GAPS

  - Gatling against small targets: left as designed (very inaccurate past
    its 35 m ideal). Gun Sniper hit rate 61-100% at 25 m, 10-31% at 50 m
    (Iron Kong 86-100% at 50 m). The gatling still lands enough rounds up
    close at 16 rounds/s. If it should be reliable on the Gun Sniper too, the
    option is about 25% less spread while the aim is snapped to a locked
    target. Not applied.
  - Turrets used to fire on their own at the lock. Under "fire only the
    selected weapon" they now fire only when selected with the trigger held
    (player and AI). They keep their own traverse and their own aim.
  - RMB on a non-sniper weapon is a second fire button (it was weapon group
    2 before).
  - Number keys 1-4 used to be held group triggers; now 1-9 select a slot.
  - Lock assist is on the locked target only and is modest (snap radius
    0.3 + 0.22 x radius m); it does not move the camera.
  - Missiles now hit stationary targets almost every time at every range.
    Moving targets still break the lock at 30 m/s as before.
  - A melee swing that is out of reach still lunges.
  - No per-mount traverse limits were added for the player (optional in the
    brief). The AI still turns its chassis within 0.6 rad to fire.
  - Hit volumes are axis-aligned boxes in the Zoid's root frame; they do not
    follow leg animation frame by frame.
  - No gamepad or touch input exists, so no left-trigger scope.
  - Damage numbers are unchanged; see 10 for the proposed x1.3 option.
