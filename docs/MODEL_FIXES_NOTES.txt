MODEL FIXES (Geno Saurer, Iron Kong, Blade Liger), 2026-10-20

All changes live in tools/optimize_models.mjs and are baked into
assets/models/{geno-saurer,iron-kong,blade-liger}.glb. Rebuild only these:
  ONLY=geno-saurer,iron-kong,blade-liger node tools/optimize_models.mjs
(ONLY is new; without it all five are rebuilt. Shield Liger and Command Wolf
were left untouched on purpose; shield-liger-anim.glb is not produced here.)

New in the optimizer
- PRE_ROT: per-model X/Y/Z rotation before the facing yaw.
  Geno Saurer [-90,0,0]: the source lay on its back (tail pointing up).
  Iron Kong [-90,-90,0]: the source lay on its back facing sideways.
  Override for experiments: PREROT_IRON_KONG="x,y,z" (degrees).
- CUSTOM_SPLIT + new targets for Geno (raptor: big head/jaw forward, small
  arms under the chest, hind legs, long tail, back cannons as backMount) and
  Kong (gorilla: head/brow, chest, long arms as left/rightWeapon, short legs,
  missile pack as backMount, rear hip armour as "tail").
- POST: per-part tint (Geno dark red body, grey guns; Kong dark grey body,
  orange missile pack/brow), Geno head and jaw scaled 1.45x about the neck,
  Blade Liger tail pitched down 0.5 rad about its root (top 3.64 -> 2.42).

Sizes in game (largest dim x catalog scale): Command Wolf ~4.5 long / 2.1
tall, Blade Liger ~6.7 long, Geno ~7.3 long / 4.5 tall, Iron Kong ~5.3 tall.
Hitbox radius and weapon anchors are per Zoid (catalog radius, anchors from
the GLB part bounds), so they follow the new splits.

Known limits
- Shield Liger, Command Wolf, Blade Liger quad splits were not re-tuned.
- Iron Kong source pose is a crouched knuckle-walk; it is not re-posed.
- Geno head scaling can overlap the neck slightly.
