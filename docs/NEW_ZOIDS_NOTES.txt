Poly Haven has no creature/mech models of this kind, and nothing for these
names was found on OpenGameArt. Sketchfab downloads also need a login.

So all five are ORIGINAL procedural models made for this project
(js/models.js: buildSniper, buildElephant, buildFuhrer, buildMolga,
buildPteras), in the same box/cone/cylinder style as the Zaber Fang,
Gojulas and Death Saurer mocks. No third-party assets. They face -Z, stand
on y = 0 (Pteras hovers), and use their own part split:

  Gun Sniper      biped. Lean forward-leaning raptor, long neck and slim head.
                  backMount = sniper rifle on a rail over the hips/tail,
                  barrel running forward past the head (90 deg turret).
                  left/rightWeapon = thigh missile pods. tail raised.
  Elephander      quad. Wide body, pillar legs, big ears, tusks (head).
                  headWeapon = trunk with a claw. left/right = shoulder
                  guns. backMount = twin back cannon turret (270 deg).
  Berserk Fuhrer  biped. Black/red T-rex, small front arms on the core,
                  left/rightWeapon = the two Buster Claws on back arms
                  reaching over the shoulders. backMount = charged particle
                  cannon (fixed, braced like Geno). Long tail.
  Molga           quad layout used for a caterpillar: three armoured body
                  segments = core, the four "legs" are the tread pods under
                  the front and rear segments, ram head = head/headWeapon,
                  small side guns = left/right, top gun = 360 deg turret.
  Pteras          biped. Small body and legs, broad wings = left/right
                  mounts (wing guns), crested head, belly rack = backMount.
                  Flies for real (section 5).

Sizes (stock, before the enemy IFF beacon): Molga and Pteras are the small
ones (Molga ~1.5 m wide, 4.4 m long; Pteras body ~3.8 m long with ~6.8 m
wingspan), Elephander the largest four-legged frame (scale 1.55).

Hangar preview: the camera now also backs off by the model's real bounding
size (js/hangar.js), so big frames like Elephander fit the stage.

Pteras flight: replaced by real flight, see section 5. (The earlier
flyHeight model offset is gone.)

5. Real flight: generic "flyer" movement mode (js/flight.js)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Any Zoid with a `flight` block in js/catalog.js flies; Pteras is the first
(flight: ceiling 70, stallSpeed 9, takeoffSpeed 9, climbRate 10,
stallSink 7, meleeHeight 4). Raynos / Storm Sworder can reuse it by adding
the same block. The physics position has a true altitude
(root.position.y); hit meshes, parts, the lock aim point and the camera all
move with it in 3D. Pitch and bank tilt only the model, the physics frame
stays upright.

Controls (player flyer; help screen H and the control strip updated):
  W / S      airspeed up / down. In the air S only slows, no reverse.
  Space      climb.            C      descend.
  Mouse / arrows  turn (the model banks into the turn).
  Shift boost, Q skill (Bombing Run: 4 bombs at the aim point), V camera,
  Tab / R lock, as before. Space and C were unbound; Ctrl was avoided
  because Ctrl+W closes the browser tab.

Flight rules:
  - Minimum airspeed, no hover. Below 9 m/s in the air it stalls: climb
    input is ignored and it sinks at up to 7 m/s until it speeds up or
    lands. On the ground it walks like a small biped; it lifts off once
    airspeed reaches 9 m/s and Space is held.
  - Climb / descend at up to 10 m/s, eased. No input holds altitude.
  - Pitch follows the climb (atan(vertical / airspeed), max 0.5 rad).
  - Ceiling: 70 m above the terrain under it.
  - Floor: the terrain, or the top of any rock or building under its
    footprint. It cannot go below the floor. Fast, it skims the floor;
    slow (under stall speed), it lands. Obstacles below its altitude do not
    block it (resolveObstacles skips them); it cannot fly through the side
    of an obstacle taller than its altitude. Unit separation ignores pairs
    more than 5 m apart vertically.
  - Crash: losing both wings (left/right mounts) or both legs makes it
    fall (gravity 18 m/s2); hitting the floor faster than 6 m/s kills it.
    Losing the core kills it as for any Zoid.
  - Melee (dash skills: Buster Claw Charge, Laser Blade, Ram) needs the
    two units within 4 m vertically, so ground melee cannot reach a flyer
    above that and a flyer must swoop low to melee. Claw / blade weapons
    are short-range hitscan, so their 8-14 m reach is already 3D.

Combat both ways:
  - AI barrels are still clamped to +-0.6 rad of yaw around the chassis,
    but elevation is now free up to 1.3 rad (aim.js clampAimYawPitch), so
    ground Zoids aim up at flyers and flyers aim down.
  - AI leads moving targets with travelling shots using the target's 3D
    velocity (climbs and dives included); hitscan beams need no lead. The
    player's ballistic LEAD bracket also uses 3D velocity.
  - Missiles lock and home onto air targets (3D homing was already there).
  - Turrets elevate to the lock (already 3D); their range check is now 3D.
    Lock-only turret rule unchanged.
  - Bombing Run (Q) drops bombs at the aim point; the belly missile pack
    fires at the lock.

AI flyers (js/ai.js): full throttle (never stall), cruise at ~22 m above
the floor when far, dive to ~13 m on the run in, break off past the target
(climb to ~24 m and turn away for 2.2 s), then swing back for another pass.
Ground AI targets and leads air targets like any other.

HUD: an ALT (m above the floor) / AIR (km/h) row under the speed bar, with
STALL or LANDED flags. Minimap: airborne contacts get an open ring around
their dot. Chase and first-person cameras follow the true altitude.

Balance: unchanged numbers on paper (balance report has no altitude term):
Pteras keeps the thinnest core (EHP 56.8) and 36 DPS at 60 m; it pays for
height with stall and crash rules and can be shot from the ground.

Tests:
  tests/flight.test.mjs (npm test): take-off needs airspeed, climb above
  20 m, level hold, 70 m ceiling, stall sinks and lands (no hover),
  terrain/obstacle floor and skimming, banking, crash rule, melee height,
  and ground fire maths (AI clamp keeps elevation, beam ray passes through
  a flyer 30 m up, a led shell meets a moving flyer).
  tests/pteras_flight_browser.mjs (npm test): in a real mission Pteras takes
  off and climbs above 20 m, the HUD shows ALT, it flies over the tallest
  obstacle without being pushed aside, damages a ground Molga from 22 m up,
  and a live ground Molga hits it at 18 m. Screenshots:
  screenshots/pteras-flight/pteras-airborne-chase.png,
  pteras-airborne-first-person.png, ground-zoid-shooting-up.png.

GUN SNIPER GLB (25 Oct 2026, follow-up)
---------------------------------------
Source: gun_sniper_daniel.glb supplied by Daniel Williams. 5,840 triangles,
0.14 MB after compression. No author or licence in the file (only the
"trimesh" generator tag), so it is assumed to be Daniel's own work. Confirm
before any public release.
Orientation: file was Y-up with feet on the floor and the nose on +X; it is
turned 90 degrees so the nose points to -Z, centred on X/Z, feet at y = 0.
Size: 4.0 units nose to tail (canon 11.9 m, about 0.34 units per metre,
inside the 0.25-0.38 band used for the other models). Game scale stays 0.92.
Parts are split by node name instead of the geometric zones: rifle = back
mount, chest beam gun = head weapon, side missile pods / thrusters / back
fins = left and right weapons, legs by side, head/jaw/canopy/sensors = head,
everything else (torso, neck, arms) = core.
The long rifle points backwards over the tail, so it uses the back-mount
turret anchor. The side hardpoints are renamed "Left/Right Missile Pod" to
match where the pods sit on the model. Stats and loadout are unchanged.
The old procedural Gun Sniper stays as the ?mock=1 fallback.
