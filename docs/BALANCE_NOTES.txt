BALANCE NOTES — Zoids, weapons, and back-mount turrets (2026-10-20)

Reference: Zoids Fanon Wiki, "Weapons Guide" by Phelan,
https://zoidsfanon.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons_Guide . It is a fan wiki, so it
was used for each weapon class's character, not for hard numbers.

All weapon numbers are in one table: WEAPONS in js/catalog.js (each entry has
a comment linking it to the guide). The Zoid stats, hardpoints (including the
turret arcs) and stock loadouts are in ZOIDS and stockWeaponsFor() in the same
file. Paper DPS / TTK: node tools/balance_report.mjs [catalog path].

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TURRETS (js/turret.js, mission.js updateTurrets)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Per-weapon option: WEAPONS[x].turret = { arc, speed } (arc up to 2*PI,
  speed rad/s). A hardpoint can also carry turret, used for whatever is
  fitted there. Only the back mount turrets; charge and melee weapons never do.
- Locked target only. Player: the Tab (cycle) / R (nearest) lock. AI: its
  own chosen target. No lock = the turret returns to its rest angle (0, the
  nose) and does NOT fire, even with an enemy close. No nearest-enemy
  fallback.
- With a lock it swings at its traverse speed (clamped to the arc; limited
  arcs never swing through the back), and auto-fires when the barrel is
  within 0.09 rad of the bearing and the lock is inside the weapon range,
  whatever the chassis heading and while moving. Turret missile packs lock
  onto the same target. Turret shots are never clamped to the nose.
- The back-mount part meshes and the weapon anchor are re-parented under a
  pivot (models.js buildTurretPivots) that visibly rotates. Skinned meshes
  (Shield Liger rig) are never re-parented.
- Turret weapons ignore the group trigger. Command Wolf's turret is on
  group 3 for the HUD only.

Reading it:
- Command Wolf: 37.0 -> 47.2 effective DPS at 60 m (+28%), 43 -> 64 at
  10 m, core 62 -> 72. Before it had the lowest staying power and its
  missiles were its only reach; now it out-damages the mediums mid-range
  while still dying fastest, and the 360 turret lets it shoot at its lock
  while running. Its weak point is now reach (120 m).
- Blade Liger: best close damage (66 at 10 m), weakest at 60 m (21.6) on
  purpose: it has to close with the blades.

  now kills its core in 2.9-3.7 s (was 3.7-4.5 s); the Blade Liger needs
  6.4 s there because it should be in blade range. The heavies stay the
  slowest to kill.

DAMAGE CLASS vs ARMOUR (added with the difficulty/escort batch)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Table: DAMAGE_VS_ARMOR in js/catalog.js (next to WEAPONS). Class comes from
weapon kind via damageClassOf(): energy -> beam, ballistic/missile ->
ballistic, melee -> melee. A weapon can override with damageClass.

  class      vs armour plating   vs structure / components
  beam       x0.70               x1.00 (current damage)
  ballistic  x1.30               x1.00 (current damage)
  melee      x1.00               x1.00

  beam:      pulseLaser, beamCannon, doubleBeamCannon, chargedParticle
  ballistic: gatling, shockCannon, missilePack (rockets count as kinetic)
  melee:     strikeLaserClaw, laserBlade

Melee is neutral on purpose: claws and blades already trade reach (8-14 m)
and per-swing heat for the highest damage per hit; a plating bonus would make
them the best tool against every armoured target, and a beam-style penalty
would make the Laser Blade pointless against fresh Zoids.

How it is applied (js/damage.js damagePart): while a part still has armour,
the armour step sees amount x armorMul. The raw amount the plating did not
absorb is converted back (armour taken / armorMul) and carried on to
structure at internalMul. So a 30 dmg beam shot into 7 armour strips it with
10 raw and puts the other 20 into structure, unchanged. The same path is
used for player and AI (hitscan and projectile hits both pass the weapon's
damage ctx into applyDamage -> applyHit). Skill hits (slash, ground pound,
artillery) carry no class and stay x1.

docs/balance_report_after_armor.txt), effective DPS at 60 m:

  commandWolf   47.2  41.1       47.2
  zaberFang     42.1  37.5       42.1
  shieldLiger   43.1  38.8       43.1
  bladeLiger    21.6  15.1       21.6
  ironKong      40.2  52.2       40.2
  genoSaurer    41.3  28.9       41.3
  gojulas       37.3  40.9       37.3
  deathSaurer   39.4  43.6       39.4

TTK at 60 m now chews the core's armour at the vs-armour rate and its
structure at the vs-component rate. Beam-heavy Zoids (Geno Saurer, Blade
Liger, Command Wolf) take longer to open a fresh Zoid (e.g. Command Wolf vs
Shield Liger 2.9 s -> 3.2 s, Geno vs Shield 3.3 -> 4.2 s); kinetic loadouts
(Iron Kong, Gojulas, Death Saurer) open plating faster (Iron Kong vs Shield
3.4 -> 2.9 s). Once plating is gone the numbers are unchanged.
