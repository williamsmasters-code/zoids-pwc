SHIELD LIGER (RZ-007) MOTION NOTES
Reference for the skeletal animations in the Zoids proof of concept.
Sources are web pages read on 2026-10-17. Notes are paraphrased, not copied.
Limits: I could not watch the anime frame by frame. Gait notes come from the
fact that it is a lion-type Zoid plus the written descriptions below.
Where I say "design choice", it is my own animation decision.

BASIC DATA [1][3]
 Lion type, Helic Republic, rollout ZAC 2042. Length 21.6 m, height 9.0 m,
 weight 92 t, top speed 250 km/h. Built to face the Zenebas Saber Tiger and
 faster than it. Weapons are folded into the body to cut drag. [1] says the
 trade-off is poorer balance at speed while the weapons are stowed.

GAIT (design choice, based on a big-cat quadruped)
 Walk: diagonal pairs move together (front left with rear right). Slow, heavy
 steps. Head and mane sway a little. Tail swings opposite to the body.
 Run: bounding gallop. The spine flexes, front legs reach forward together and
 rear legs push together, with a short airborne moment. Head stays level so the
 weapons remain aimed forward. Weapons stay stowed while running [1].
 Idle: slow breathing motion in the torso and a small tail drift.

ENERGY SHIELD DEPLOYMENT [1][2][4][5]
 - The generators are built into the mane. The top and bottom mane panels
   expand. [1]
 - In the anime the sides of the mane also flash gold. The field builds up in
   planes and then solidifies into a dome. It wraps the top and sides and is
   strongest at the front. [1][2]
 - Blog description [5]: a chin armor plate flips down at roughly 70 degrees
   while a plate behind the cockpit flips up to the same angle. The shield
   covers the front half of the Zoid.
 - Shields can be used to ram. Two shields that collide cancel each other. [1][4]
 - Heavy hits can overload the shield (Geno Saurer's charged particle cannon
   broke it in the series [2]). The game's heat lockout is based on this.
 Animation plan: ShieldActivate is a one-shot. The lower mane/chin plate drops
 about 70 degrees, the upper plate behind the head rises about 70 degrees, the
 side panels flare outward, and the body braces (lower head and chest, legs set
 wide). ShieldMaintain loops: panels hold their pose with a small hum-like
 vibration and a slow breathing sway.

WEAPONS AND HOW THEY FIRE [1][2][3][5]
 - Upper back: AMD two-barrel 20 mm beam gun, tucked under a back armor panel.
   The panel flips up to expose it. [1][5] Tail: 30 mm two-barrel beam gun
   (standard model only; Bang's version lacks it). [1][2]
 - Chest: Anti-Zoid triple shock cannon, three barrels, three shots, usually
   shown firing one at a time. [1]
 - Sides: two folding missile pods that swing down from the flanks. [1][5]
 - Mouth: two laser sabres. Paws: four strike claws. [1]
 Animation plan: Fire is a one-shot. The back armor panel flips up, the twin
 gun pushes back (recoil) and returns, the chest cannon kicks back and the whole
 body rocks back a little, then everything returns to idle. The game also uses
 this clip for any weapon fire. Not modeled: separate missile pod deploy,
 sabre, claws (the model's meshes do not separate them).

NOTE ON THE GAME'S SHIELD
 In the game the shield is a frontal energy arc. Weapons cannot fire while it
 is up. The Fire clip is therefore never played during the shield.
