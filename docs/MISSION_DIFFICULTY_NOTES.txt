MISSION DIFFICULTY + SUPPLY PUSH (ESCORT) NOTES
===============================================

1. Per-mission difficulty (js/difficulty.js, one table)
--------------------------------------------------------
Click a contract -> launch popover: brief, difficulty slider 1-5, live
payout, live hostile list and AI numbers, "Launch with <active Zoid>".
Moving the slider updates the popover payout and the contract buttons at
once. The value is saved (state.missionDifficulty in the save) and every
contract uses it until changed; a new company uses each contract's
suggested level. The old fixed difficulty dots are gone; buttons show
"D3 Veteran".

 AI aim: the pilot's aim point wanders by +-aimError per 100 m of range.

 (withDifficulty in js/contracts.js), so what the popover lists is what you
 fight.

2. Supply Push / convoy escort (js/escort.js, js/mission.js, js/ai.js)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Route: trucks start at z=+310 and drive to the EXIT at z=-300 (610 m,
  ~76 s at 8 m/s). Player starts at z=+290, 20 m ahead of the trucks so they
  are never in the line of fire (was: trucks 28 m behind the player and the
  first wave spawned 32-50 m in front of the player). A start message gives
  the reported window ("Raiders reported 244-427 m up the 610 m route").
- Ambush: wave 1 spawns at a seeded random 40-70% of the route, 12-60 m off
  the road; wave 2 (reinforcements) 15-25% further on (max 88%). So the
  nearest raider starts ~245-430 m from the trucks (30-53 s of truck travel)
  and 220+ m from the player, who reaches it in ~12-20 s at ~75 km/h.
- Raiders hold their ambush spot (guard radius 120 m) until a truck or the
  player comes inside it or someone shoots them.
- Target choice (js/ai.js chooseTarget): 1. whoever shot it within the last
  8 s, 2. the player if inside the aggro range (table above), 3. the trucks,
  4. nearest foe. So shooting a raider or getting close pulls it off the
  convoy (distraction works) and it drifts back to the trucks if ignored.
- Trucks: armour and structure x truckArmor (table above).
- Warning: once the ambush is inside the player's radar range (230 m with a
  healthy head) or the lead truck is within 150 m of it, red AMBUSH markers
  with live distance appear (screen-edge arrow + minimap) and the message
  "Ambush spotted N m out. Trucks are M m from it." The objective line then
  shows the ambush distance ahead of the convoy.

3. Escort sim (tests/escort_browser.mjs)
---------------------------------------
Bot pilot (window.__ZOIDS__.autopilot, test-only) drives to the nearest
raider, locks it, stands off at its longest optimal range and strafes.
Fixed-step sim (stepSim 0.05 s), seed 1002 "Road Open".
  D1  starter Shield Liger (pulse laser, beam cannon, gatling): WIN,
      2/2 trucks, 2/2 kills, 77 s. Ambush 344 m from trucks at start.
  D3  same Liger, plating +2: WIN, 2/2 trucks, 3/3 kills, 77 s.
      Ambush 340 m from trucks.
  D5  plated Geno Saurer (+ charged particle): LOSS, 0/2 trucks left,
      1/3 kills in 50 s. Ambush 275 m from trucks.
  (Final run 21 Oct 2026: D1 WIN 2/2 trucks 77 s, D3 WIN 2/2 trucks 76 s,
  D5 LOSS 0 trucks 1/3 kills 50 s.)
  In every run the first raider switched to the player while still
  ~230-260 m from the trucks (when the bot closed to ~130-150 m).
The test asserts the D1 win and reports D3/D5. D5 losing to a simple bot
is expected for "Ace"; it is not proof it is unwinnable by a human.

4. Test-harness notes
---------------------
- headless.mjs: the first contract is an escort. It now checks the ambush
  starts >= 200 m from the trucks and holds, then walks the player up to the
  raiders for the "AI moves" check and puts the player back.
- headless.mjs lock (2.2 s) and charge checks now run on fixed sim steps
  (stepSim) and measure game seconds. They were wall-clock based and failed
  when an enemy model costs more to render under swiftshader (a recon run
  with a Command Wolf enemy rendered at ~12 ms/step vs ~5 ms for a Zaber
  Fang).
- glb_browser.mjs average-frame guard (< 100 ms): measured 85-88 ms in the
  final full run and 96-106 ms in earlier runs on the same box (HEAD 0beafc5
  measured 78 ms in the same period, 55-61 ms earlier in the day), so this
  check is close to its limit on this machine.
