WEAPONS OVERHAUL NOTES
Zoids Mercenaries, November 2026

This pass reworks how weapons aim, fire, travel and lose power, in a
MechWarrior 5 style. Every Zoid keeps its existing loadout; the per-shot
damage of every weapon is unchanged, and the sustained damage per second of
every weapon is the same as before. What changed is how hard it is to land
that damage, and where.

All the rules live in one file, js/weaponsys.js, which has no three.js in it,
so the node tests and the game use exactly the same maths. The player and
the AI pilots go through the same functions.


1. TARGETING

Sensor lock range
  190 m, multiplied by the Zoid's sensor quality and any radar skill. You can
  only lock enemies inside it. The target panel still says OUT OF SENSOR
  RANGE for a contact you can see but not lock.

Targeting box
  R locks the enemy closest to the crosshair inside a 12 degree box around
  the crosshair. If nobody is inside the box, R locks the nearest enemy in
  sensor range. If nobody is in range at all, you get "No contacts in sensor
  range".

Target cycling
  Tab steps through enemies inside sensor range, nearest first, and wraps.

Lock loss
  A dead target drops at once. A target outside sensor range keeps your lock
  for 3 seconds, then the lock drops with "Target lock lost". Coming back
  into range inside the 3 seconds resets the timer.

At mission start you are only locked if an enemy is already inside sensor
range (before, the nearest enemy was locked from any distance).

Turrets still fire ONLY at the locked target, exactly as before.

Missile seeker cone
  Missiles lock on the enemy under the crosshair, or on your locked target if
  it sits inside the weapon's 8 degree seeker cone (was 6 degrees). The cone
  is drawn as a faint dashed circle round the crosshair whenever a missile
  weapon is fitted, and it fills as the lock builds.


2. RETICLE AND HUD

  Bloom circle   The crosshair circle grows with group 1's current spread:
                 sustained fire, moving and hot barrels all widen it. It
                 shrinks back when you stop.
  Group pips     Four small ticks round the circle (top = group 1, right = 2,
                 bottom = 3, left = 4), each coloured by that group's range
                 band on the target: green ideal, yellow long, orange extreme
                 or short, red out of range.
  Range          The number under the circle is the distance to whatever the
                 crosshair is on.
  Target box     Corner brackets round the locked enemy with its range,
                 coloured by group 1's band.
  IMPACT         For ballistic guns, a diamond where the shell will REALLY
                 land at the crosshair distance.
  DROP / ZERO    How far below the crosshair that is ("DROP 4.0m"), or
                 "ZERO 45m" when you are inside the gun's zero range and the
                 shell meets the crosshair.
  LEAD           A box showing where to put the crosshair to hit the locked
                 enemy if it keeps moving, with shell travel time AND drop
                 worked in.
  Warnings       "BEAM SCATTER, DUST 20 m" when your line of fire crosses a
                 cloud with a beam weapon fitted, and "SEEKER BLOCKED, DUST"
                 when a cloud stops your missile lock.


3. FIRE MODES AND FIRING SPEED

Heat in the catalog is now per CYCLE. A pulse or a salvo splits that heat
evenly over its shots.

  auto    Gatling Gun. Spins up from 35% of its rate to the full 16 rounds a
          second over 0.6 s, and spins down when released.
  single  Shock Cannon (0.5 a second), Sniper Rifle (0.55 a second). One
          heavy shell per pull.
  pulse   Pulse Laser. Bursts of 3 pulses 0.07 s apart, 2.5 bursts a second.
          Once a burst starts it finishes even if you let go.
  beam    Beam Cannon and the Double-Barreled Beam Cannon turret. A sustained
          beam you have to hold on the target: the Beam Cannon's 18 damage is
          spread over a 0.45 s beam, the twin turret's over 0.3 s. Damage
          lands every frame the beam touches something.
  salvo   Missile Pack. A ripple of 4 missiles 0.14 s apart, one salvo every
          2.5 s, only once the lock is complete.
  charge  Charged Particle Cannon. Unchanged: hold 3 s while standing still,
          then it fires on its own.
  melee   Claws and blades. Unchanged.

Losing a weapon mid-burst (overheat or a destroyed mount) cancels the rest
of the burst.


4. SPREAD

Every shot scatters inside a cone. The cone width is:

  (base spread x range-band spread + bloom + movement) x heat haze x skill

  Base spread       per weapon (table below).
  Range-band spread ballistics widen past ideal: x3 at the long-range mark
                    and x6 at max range, smoothly in between. Beams barely
                    widen (x1.1, x1.25).
  Bloom             each shot adds a little, up to a cap; it recovers when
                    you stop firing. The gatling blooms most.
  Movement          extra spread at full speed: 0.014 rad for guns, 0.004
                    for beams, 0.03 for the Sniper Rifle (it hates moving),
                    none for missiles.
  Heat haze         above 60% weapon heat the barrel shimmers: up to x1.35
                    for guns and x1.5 for beams at 100%.
  Skill             braced skills (Iron Kong and friends) still tighten it.


5. BALLISTIC WEAPONS: TRAVEL, DROP AND FALLOFF

Shells travel at their own speed and fall under gravity, as before. The big
change is ZEROING:

  Every gun is zeroed at its ideal (optimal) range. Inside that range the
  barrel lifts just enough for the shell to meet the crosshair. Past it, the
  barrel keeps the zero-range lift, so the shell crosses the line of sight at
  the zero distance and then lands LOW. Before this pass the barrel always
  solved the full arc, so drop never mattered.

  Drop past zero (flat ground, shooting at a target at the same height):
    Gatling Gun    zero 35 m: about 0.4 m low at 65 m, 1.35 m low at 100 m
    Shock Cannon   zero 45 m: about 1.8 m low at 75 m, 4.3 m low at 100 m,
                   4.9 m low at 105 m (max range)
    Sniper Rifle   zero 140 m: about 0.4 m low at 190 m, 0.9 m low at 230 m

  So with the Shock Cannon at 100 m you aim about one Zoid-height high, or
  put the LEAD box on the target. The HUD's IMPACT diamond and DROP number
  tell you exactly where the shell goes.

  Heavy shells (Shock Cannon, Sniper Rifle) leave a 0.9 s tracer so you can
  see the arc; the gatling shows every 4th round.

  Shock Cannon shells are slightly faster (80 m/s, was 75) and a little less
  heavy (gravity 10, was 12) so the drop is readable rather than absurd.

Damage falloff (all non-melee weapons)
  Damage is now a smooth curve rather than steps:
    full damage up to the ideal range,
    a straight line down to the long-range mark,
    a straight line down to max range,
    nothing past max range.
  Ballistic:  1.0 at ideal, 0.85 at the long mark, 0.70 at max.
  Beam:       1.0 at ideal, 0.30 at the long mark, 0.10 at max.
  Missile:    1.0 at ideal, 0.95 at the long mark, 0.85 at max.
  Weapons with a minimum range (Sniper Rifle, Charged Particle Cannon) ramp
  up from 85% / 75% at point blank to full at their minimum range. The range
  band labels (IDEAL / LONG / EXTREME / SHORT) are unchanged and still used
  for the HUD, AI aim error and missile lock time.


6. ENERGY WEAPONS: FALLOFF AND BEAM REFRACTION

Beams are instant and lose power fast past their ideal range (curve above).

Refraction
  Every map now has 3 or 4 clouds of its biome's kind (kept off the spawn
  and convoy lane), and every destroyed Zoid leaves a smoke column that burns
  for about 25 s (at most 4 at once):
    Desert   DUST     soaks 2.4% of a beam per metre, bends it 0.0014 rad/m
    Plains   SMOKE    soaks 3.0% per metre, bends 0.0018 rad/m (thickest)
    Arctic   ICE FOG  soaks 2.0% per metre, bends 0.0012 rad/m
    Wrecks   SMOKE

  When a beam enters a cloud it bends at the entry point by up to the bend
  figure times the cloud path (capped at 0.08 rad, about 4.6 degrees), in a
  random direction, and throws a spray of short scattered rays you can see.
  Its damage is multiplied by the light that survives the cloud path
  (never below 20%).

  Example: 20 m of dust leaves a Pulse Laser about 62% of its damage and
  bends it up to about 1.6 degrees, so at the far side some pulses miss.

  How much each weapon cares ("refract"): Pulse Laser 1.0, Double Beam
  Cannon 0.85, Beam Cannon 0.8, Charged Particle Cannon 0.35 (a particle
  stream punches through; 20 m of dust only takes 15%). Shells, missiles and
  melee are not refracted at all, which is the point: in a dust storm,
  bring a gun.

Energy shields
  A beam that hits a raised Shield Liger shield is scattered: you see a
  burst of rays off the shield. (The shield already blocked the damage.)

Charged Particle Cannon
  Keeps its full-charge mechanic exactly as before.


7. MISSILES

  Lock          2.2 s on a still target (unchanged), longer at long and
                extreme range and against fast movers; 30 m/s or more breaks
                it (unchanged). The seeker cone is 8 degrees.
  Lock decay    Slipping off the target drains the lock twice as fast as it
                builds (it used to drain at the build rate).
  Arming        Warheads arm after 25 m. A hit closer than that does 25%.
  Salvo         4 missiles rippled 0.14 s apart.
  Countermeasures
                A cloud on the line of sight with 12 m or more of thickness
                blocks the lock (SEEKER BLOCKED). A target standing inside a
                cloud blinds missiles already in flight: their turning power
                drops to 30%, so they tend to miss. Moving fast still
                breaks the lock as before.


8. AI USES THE SAME RULES

  Same spread, bloom, movement and heat haze.
  Same zeroing and drop. AI pilots hold over by a skill share that rises
  with difficulty: Green 0.45, Regular 0.65, Veteran 0.8, Elite 0.9, Ace 1.0
  (1.0 = perfect compensation). So a Green pilot's shells land well short at
  long range; an Ace's land where he aims.
  Same falloff and refraction, same missile lock rules (cone, cloud block,
  speed break, arming).
  Each AI weapon only fires with the target inside that weapon's own max
  range, and missiles only outside their 25 m arming range.
  The existing difficulty knobs (aim error, reaction, fire rate, plating)
  are unchanged.
  The player's own turrets get a fire-control computer with 0.9 holdover.


9. BALANCE

Per-shot damage and sustained DPS are unchanged for every weapon. The paper
balance report (docs/balance_report_weapons_overhaul.txt, before and after)
shows time-to-kill at 60 m moving by 0-15% for most pairs, mostly from the
smooth falloff giving beams a little more just past their ideal range. The
Blade Liger gains most (its pulse lasers at 60 m are just past ideal):
12.8 s to 7.4 s against a Command Wolf on paper. The paper model does not
include bloom, movement, drop or clouds, which pull real hit rates back
down, so live time-to-kill should land close to before. The whole roster
still spans roughly 2 to 20 s on paper.

12. SEPARATE FIX: THIRD SPEED BOOSTER LOST ON RELOAD

js/state.js capped every mod level at 2 when a save loaded. Speed Boosters go
to 3, so the third booster (90,000 C) vanished after a refresh. The caps now
come from each mod's own max in js/catalog.js (Speed Boosters 3, Maneuver
Gyros 2, Back Boosters 2). Covered by the last check in
tests/weapons_overhaul.test.mjs, which fails on the old code.


13. SMALL SIDE CHANGES

  Pacified test missions (a test-only mode) now still fly the player's
  shells, so shell tests and screenshots work. Normal play is unaffected.
  Skill shots without range bands (Geno Saurer's Q bolt) now do full damage
  out to their range; before, they were read as the ballistic "long" band
  (90%).
