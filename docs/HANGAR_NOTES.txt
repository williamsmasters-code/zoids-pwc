HANGAR REWORK NOTES
===================

Layout (js/hangar.js, css/style.css)
- The left roster column is gone. A "Viewing" dropdown (#zoid-select) sits in
  the top bar. Each entry reads "Name - role - owned | price | owned · ACTIVE".
  Picking an unowned Zoid shows a Buy button (stage banner and loadout title),
  so buying still works.
- The 3D preview has the whole centre column. The dossier is a compact strip
  under the canvas (name, role, km/h, deg/s, boost, sensors, tonnage bar). The
  "Details" button opens the blurb, warnings, weapon heat/range lines and
  skills below the strip. The strip and the details sit in normal flow, so the
  canvas shrinks instead of being covered. headless.mjs checks that the canvas
  bottom is above the dossier top.
- Contracts are three compact buttons (.contract-btn.launch): name, payout,
  type · location, difficulty dots. One click launches the contract. The full
  brief is in the hover tooltip (title attribute).
- The right column (loadout, Set active, Sell, repair, hardpoints, armour,
  mods, shop, inventory) is unchanged.
- The "Model credits" button and the CC-BY note are kept in the footer.

Selected (viewing) vs active
- Not a bug and the logic is unchanged (Daniel confirmed). state.selected is
  the active Zoid that launches on contracts. Browsing another Zoid only changes
  what is viewed.
- To make this clear: the dropdown marks "owned · ACTIVE", the top bar shows
  "Active: <name>", and a banner over the stage reads either "ACTIVE - this
  Zoid launches on contracts" or "VIEWING - not active, contracts launch with
  <active> [Set <name> active]". The contracts header says "Launches with
  <active> (you are viewing <name>)".

Tonnage zones (js/tonnage.js, one table)
  load = fitted weight / rated tonnage (def.maxTonnage)

  zone         load         speed       accel       turn        cooling
  Light        0 - 80%      x1.00       x1.00       x1.00       x1.00
  Loaded       80 - 100%    1.00->0.90  1.00->0.85  1.00->0.90  x1.00
  Overloaded   100 - 120%   0.90->0.65  0.85->0.55  0.90->0.65  0.85->0.70
  Critical     120 - 130%   0.65->0.55  0.55->0.45  0.65->0.55  0.70->0.60
  Ceiling      above 130%   fitting refused; launch blocked

- Inside a zone the values ease linearly, so there is no cliff at a boundary.
  The one deliberate step is cooling, which drops to x0.85 the moment the
  Zoid becomes overloaded.
- The old hard cap (launch blocked above 100%) is gone. Overloaded and
  Critical Zoids can launch.
- Ceiling: equip, armour + and mod + are refused when the result would go
  over 130% ("Over the 130% tonnage ceiling. Remove gear first."). A legacy
  save that is already over the ceiling cannot launch until gear is removed.
- Applied in play: derivePerformance (js/damage.js) multiplies speed and turn
  and exposes accelMul and coolMul. drivePlayer and driveAI (js/mission.js)
  scale their approach (acceleration) rates by accelMul. The weapon cooling
  loop multiplies cooling by coolMul for every unit.
- Hangar bar: four coloured bands across 0-130%, a white tick at 100% (rated),
  a fill and pointer for the current load, the zone label, and live effective
  speed and turn with their multipliers, plus accel and cooling.

Tests
- New tests/tonnage.test.mjs (7 checks): zone tiling, zone ids, the
  penalty values, monotonic and continuous multipliers, derivePerformance
  wiring, overloaded launch allowed, ceiling refusal, legacy over-ceiling
  launch block. Added to npm test.
- headless.mjs uses the dropdown and contract buttons and checks the layout
  (no roster, 3 contract buttons, 4 bands, zone label and effective values,
  no overlap with the model, active chip and banner, credits button).
- glb_browser.mjs and shield_anim_browser.mjs pick Zoids with the dropdown.
- headless.mjs overheat check: this was already flaky at HEAD 019c3b1 (1 of 2
  runs failed in a clean worktree, heat stuck around 86-96 because sim time
  depended on swiftshader frame rate). It now drives the sim with stepSim in
  fixed 1/60 s steps and stops at the overheat.
